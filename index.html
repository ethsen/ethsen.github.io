Selected Projects A window into my work

I am a graduate student pursuing an M.S.E. in Robotics at the University of Pennsylvania with a B.A. in Computational Physics from Franklin & Marshall College. My concentration lies in deep learning, computer vision, and robotics. I previously interned at Nokia Bell Labs, where I worked on IMU-based localization (RoNIN) and trajectory correction for human tracking, and at Anduril Industries, where I built infrared optical test rigs and automation tooling. Earlier, I conducted physics research focused on optical instrumentation and laser beam profiling, building interfaces and analysis tools for lab experiments. For fun, I like to work on projects that blend deep learning with non-traditional fields such as sports, fashion, and music.
